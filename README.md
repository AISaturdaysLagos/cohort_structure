# TRI AI Saturdays Lagos

**TRI AI** (Teaching · Research · Innovation), formerly AI Saturdays Lagos, has been running free AI and machine learning education across Africa since January 6, 2018. What started as a local Lagos initiative has grown into a pan-African learning community, with thousands of participants across dozens of countries, all learning together at no cost.

Each cohort is a structured, time-bound programme, typically 15 to 16 weeks, covering topics from data science fundamentals and classical machine learning to deep learning and large language models. Formats have evolved over time: from in-person and live classes, to flipped classroom models, to large-scale online cohorts in partnership with global organisations like Google DeepMind.

This repository is the central home for cohort documentation. It contains the structure, curriculum, resources, and guidelines for each cohort we have run from cohort 7 till date. Whether you are a current participant looking for materials, a prospective learner exploring what to expect, or a community looking to replicate our model, this is the right place to start.
