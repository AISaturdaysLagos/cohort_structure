## 🚀 Getting Started

New to the cohort? Head to the **[Onboarding page](https://aisaturdayslagos.github.io/cohort_structure/cohort10/onboarding.html)** and complete these steps before **May 31**:

1. Create a [Google Cloud Skills Boost](https://www.skills.google/users/sign_up) account (free)
2. Accept the cohort invitation sent to your email
3. [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/8sA4Tkgpkt) — our main community hub
4. [Join the mailing list](https://groups.google.com/a/tri-ai.org/g/cohort-10) — weekly updates & reminders
5. Register for [Saturday lectures](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/zf3nL38vSLq47X8CLt3VrA#/registration) and [Wednesday labs](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/SpBIuun4Q52q0ohtt3ltHA#/registration) on Zoom

## 🏅 Certificate Requirements

To earn your certificate of completion you must:

- Attend **≥ 60%** of Saturday lectures (tracked via the [attendance form](https://aisaturdayslagos.github.io/cohort_structure/cohort10/attendance.html))
- Complete the **"Train A Small Language Model"** challenge lab
- Submit all **four cohort challenges** as a team
- Participate in the **final capstone project** as a team
