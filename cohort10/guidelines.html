Expectations
How we show up
✅
Respect others
  • Be kind and professional at all times
  • Respect different backgrounds and skill levels — everyone starts somewhere
  • Encourage questions; there are no stupid ones
💪
Participate actively
  • Attend live sessions as consistently as you can
  • Engage in discussions on Discord and in class
  • Ask questions and support your teammates
🎯
Stay accountable
  • Complete tasks and assignments on time
  • Communicate early when you are facing challenges
  • Follow through on commitments to your team
📖
Learn publicly
  • Share insights and discoveries with the group
  • Help others when you can — teaching deepens your own understanding
  • Document your learning journey
Zero tolerance
What will not be tolerated
The following behaviours undermine the community and will result in a warning or removal from the cohort.
  • Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind
  • Hate speech or offensive language
  • Spam or excessive self-promotion in community channels
  • Academic dishonesty or plagiarism on assignments or projects
  • Disruptive behaviour during live sessions or labs
  • Sharing session recordings or materials outside the cohort without permission

Badges & recognition
How we celebrate progress
We recognise consistency, collaboration, improvement, leadership, and effort — not just raw performance. Badges are designed to encourage growth, not unhealthy competition.
🏆 Quiz Master ⭐ Attendance Star 🤝 Community Builder 📈 Most Improved 🥊 Team of the Week 🩹 Accountability Champion
Support & accountability
When someone goes quiet
If a participant becomes inactive, repeatedly misses sessions, or violates these guidelines, we follow this process — starting with care, not punishment.
1
Friendly check-in — A team member reaches out to see if everything is okay and if there is anything we can help with.
2
Direct reminder and support — We follow up with resources or adjustments to help the participant re-engage.
3
Mentor intervention — A mentor works with the participant directly to understand barriers and find a path forward.
4
Final warning or removal — If the behaviour continues or guidelines are violated, the participant may be removed from the cohort.

The goal of this cohort is not just to finish a curriculum.
It is to help you think deeply, build confidently, collaborate meaningfully, and grow into a capable AI practitioner.

Everyone starts somewhere.
Consistency, curiosity, and community matter most.
